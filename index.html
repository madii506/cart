06 — FAQ

Straight answers.

What is CART?

A memecoin on pump.fun that spends its own creator fees on TikTok ads. Every trade pays the fee, the fee funds the next campaign, and every campaign is posted on this page.

Where does the money come from?

pump.fun's creator fee: a cut of every $CART buy and sell goes to the coin's creator wallet. That wallet is the ad wallet, and its address is published here the moment the coin is live.

Who runs the ads?

A person. Buying TikTok ads takes an ad account and a card, which a coin can't hold, so someone on the team runs each campaign from the ad wallet's money. The ledger shows exactly what was spent and what it bought.

What can the ads say?

Only what TikTok's ad policies allow. Those policies restrict financial and crypto promotion, so the ads sell the meme, not the token: no prices, no "buy" calls, no promises.

How do I know the fees went to ads?

The ledger lists every campaign with its date, its spend, the ad itself and its results, with links. The wallet section reads the balance straight from Solana, so the money in and the money out can be checked against each other.

Why hasn't an ad run yet?

TikTok sets a minimum budget for every campaign, so the fees build up in the wallet until there's enough for one. Then it runs, and it lands on the ledger.

Is CART part of TikTok?

No. CART is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok. TikTok is a trademark of its owner.

Is $CART live?

Not yet. When it is, the contract address appears at the top of this page and in the pinned post on X. Anyone posting an address before that is scamming you.
